Privacy statement

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who is responsible?

Verstappe Interim & Consultancy (Joep Verstappe, Dutch Chamber of Commerce no. 89596447) is responsible for processing personal data as described in this statement.

What data do I process?

When you contact me by email or book an appointment through the calendar, I process the data you provide yourself: name, company name, email address, phone number (if provided) and the content of your message.

What do I use your data for?

Solely to answer your question, to schedule an intro call and, if we start working together, to carry out the assignment. I do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties unless required by law.

How long do I keep your data?

No longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was provided. Correspondence without follow-up is deleted no later than twelve months after the last contact, unless a legal retention obligation applies.

Booking an appointment

I use Cal.com to schedule intro calls. If you book an appointment through the calendar on this website, Cal.com processes the data you enter there (such as name and email address) to record the appointment. Cal.com's privacy policy also applies.

Cookies

This website itself does not place any tracking or marketing cookies. Your cookie choice is stored only locally in your browser. The embedded Cal.com calendar may place functional and analytical cookies; it is therefore only loaded after you give consent. If you choose essential only, you can open the calendar in a separate window. You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the website.

Visitor statistics

For anonymous visitor statistics I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It works without cookies and without tracking individual visitors.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data. To do so, email joep@verstappe.com. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
